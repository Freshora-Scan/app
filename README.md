🍋 Welcome to Freshora!

AI-Powered Food Scanner

Welcome to Freshora! 👋

Freshora is an AI-powered app that lets you take a picture of food and get an AI-generated analysis of what the image appears to show.

Whether you're curious about a food item, experimenting with AI, or simply want to see what Freshora can recognize, getting started is easy.

This guide will walk you through everything you need to know as a first-time user.

---

🚀 Getting Started

When you open Freshora for the first time, you'll see the Home screen.

From here, you can start scanning food immediately.

You'll usually see options such as:

📷 Scan Food

Take a new picture using your camera.

🖼️ Upload Photo

Choose a food picture that is already saved on your device.

📋 History

See the food scans you've made previously.

⚙️ Settings

Change available app preferences.

❓ Help

Learn more about using Freshora.

---

📷 Your First Scan

Let's try your first scan!

Step 1 — Find Some Food

Choose a food item that you want Freshora to analyze.

For your first try, choose something that's:

- Clearly visible
- Well lit
- Not hidden behind other objects
- Easy to photograph

For example, you could scan a whole apple, banana, tomato, or another food supported by the app.

---

Step 2 — Tap “Scan Food”

From the home screen, tap:

📷 Scan Food

If Freshora asks for camera permission, you'll need to allow camera access if you want to take a photograph directly inside the app.

---

Step 3 — Take Your Picture

Point your camera at the food.

Try to make sure the food is easy to see.

You don't need to take a perfect photograph. Just avoid extremely dark, blurry, or obstructed images.

When you're ready, take the picture.

---

💡 How to Take a Better Picture

A better picture can give the AI more useful information.

💡 Use enough light

Make sure your food isn't completely dark.

Natural light or evenly distributed indoor lighting usually works well.

📱 Hold your phone steady

Try not to move the phone while taking the picture.

🔎 Keep the food visible

Make sure the food you're scanning is actually visible in the photograph.

🧹 Keep distractions away

If possible, don't surround the food with lots of unrelated objects.

📐 Don't stand too far away

The food should be large enough in the photograph for the AI to see its visual features.

---

🖼️ Don't Want to Use Your Camera?

That's completely fine.

You can use an existing photograph instead.

Tap:

🖼️ Upload Photo

Then choose an image from your device.

Once you've selected the image, Freshora will use it for the analysis.

---

🤖 What Happens Next?

Once you submit your image, Freshora starts analyzing it.

You'll see an Analyzing... screen.

During this step, the AI examines the image and looks for visual patterns it has learned from its training data.

Depending on the version of Freshora you're using, this may take a few seconds.

When the analysis is finished, you'll see your result.

---

📊 Understanding Your Result

Your result screen may contain several pieces of information.

For example:

🍎 Food

Freshora may say:

«Apple»

This is the AI's prediction about what appears in the image.

---

📈 Confidence

You may also see something like:

«87% confidence»

This tells you how strongly the AI model supports its prediction.

Important:

87% confidence does NOT mean there is an 87% chance the food is safe to eat.

It only relates to the AI's prediction.

The AI can still be wrong, even when its confidence is high.

---

👀 What Does “Fresh-Looking” Mean?

Depending on the version of Freshora, the app may provide visual observations about the food.

For example, you might see a description such as:

«Fresh-looking»

This means the food has visual characteristics that the AI associates with the corresponding category.

It does not mean:

«“This food is definitely safe to eat.”»

That's an important difference.

---

⚠️ Important: Freshora Cannot Guarantee Food Safety

Before you use Freshora, there's one thing you should understand.

A picture cannot tell you everything about food.

Some food-safety problems cannot be seen in a photograph.

For example, an image cannot reliably tell you whether food contains:

- Harmful bacteria
- Viruses
- Toxins
- Certain contaminants
- Unsafe levels of microorganisms

A photograph also doesn't tell the AI:

- How long the food has been sitting out
- How it was stored
- What temperature it was stored at
- Whether it was handled safely
- Whether it has been contaminated in a way that isn't visible

Because of this:

Never use Freshora alone to decide whether food is safe to eat.

Freshora is an AI visual-analysis tool, not a food-safety test.

---

🧠 Why Does Freshora Sometimes Get Things Wrong?

Freshora learns from examples.

Just like a person can mistake one food for another when seeing a blurry photograph, an AI can make mistakes too.

For example, the AI may have trouble with:

- Unusual varieties of food
- Strange lighting
- Blurry photographs
- Partially hidden food
- Unusual angles
- Multiple foods in one picture
- Objects that look similar to food

So don't be surprised if you occasionally get an unexpected result.

---

🔄 What If My Result Looks Wrong?

That's okay!

Try scanning the food again.

Try this:

1. Take another picture

Use a different angle.

2. Improve the lighting

Make sure the food is clearly visible.

3. Move closer

Make the food easier to see.

4. Remove distractions

Try to keep other objects out of the photograph.

5. Compare the results

If Freshora gives different answers, don't assume that either answer is definitely correct.

The difference may simply mean that the AI is uncertain.

Try this:

Experiment 1

Take a clear picture of a food.

Scan it.

Experiment 2

Take another picture of the same food from a different angle.

Scan it again.

Experiment 3

💡 A Few Things to Remember

✅ Freshora can help you:

- Identify supported foods
- Explore image-based AI
- Analyze visible characteristics
- Experiment with computer vision
- Learn how AI predictions work

❌ Freshora cannot reliably:

- Detect every type of contamination
- Determine whether food is safe to eat
- Know how food was stored
- Know how long food has been sitting out
- Replace professional food-safety testing

---

🔐 Your Privacy

Before uploading photographs, remember that images can contain more than just food.

For example, a photograph might accidentally include:

- People
- Faces
- Documents
- Addresses
- Personal belongings
- Other private information

Avoid uploading images containing sensitive information unless you understand how the version of Freshora you're using handles those images.

---

🆘 Something Isn't Working?

If the app isn't behaving as expected, try:

1. Closing and reopening Freshora.
2. Trying another image.
3. Checking your camera permissions.
4. Checking that the image is properly loaded.
5. Trying the scan again.

If there's still a problem, make a note of what happened and report it to the Freshora development team.
