




Freshora

Your AI Food Scanner

Welcome to Freshora!

Freshora helps you scan food using your phone camera. It uses AI to give you a quick analysis of what is in the picture.

How to use Freshora

1. Open Freshora

Tap **Scan Food** from the home screen.

2. Take a photo

Keep the food clearly visible and make sure there is enough light. Try not to take a photo that is too blurry.

3. Wait for the AI

Freshora will analyse the image and show the result. This may take a few seconds.

4. Check the result

You may see:

- Food name
- AI confidence
- Visual observations

You can also check your previous scans from the **History** section.

*For better results

Try to:

- Keep the food clearly visible
- Use good lighting
- Hold your phone steady
- Avoid having too many things in the background

If the result looks wrong, try scanning the food again with a better photo. Sometimes the AI can make mistakes.

*Important*

Freshora cannot tell you for sure if food is safe to eat.

The AI can only analyse what it sees in the picture. It cannot detect bacteria, toxins, poor storage conditions, or other things that can make food unsafe.

Because of this, you should not use Freshora as the only reason to decide whether you should eat something.

*That’s it*

Take a photo, let Freshora analyse it, and check the result.

Have fun trying it out!
