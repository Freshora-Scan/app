🍃 Welcome to Freshora!

Freshora helps you scan food using your phone camera. It uses AI to give you a quick analysis of what is in the picture.

---

📱 How to use Freshora

01. First Launch

On first launch, enter a name or nickname and follow the eight-step tutorial.

02. Scan Your Food

Use Freshora only for fruit and vegetable photos. From Home, choose Open camera or Add photo; the Scan tab is also available in the bottom navigation.

03. Save Your Results

Save a result to keep it in History.

04. Personalise Your Experience

Settings contains theme choices, name editing, and the rate / feedback forms.

05. Confidence & Reliability

Freshora blocks a scan unless both food-identification confidence and freshness confidence are at least 80% and the model's additional reliability checks pass.

A blocked scan displays both confidence values and asks you to try again.

---

⚠️ Important

«Freshora cannot tell you for sure if food is safe to eat.»

The AI can only analyse what it sees in the picture. It cannot detect bacteria, toxins, poor storage conditions, or other things that can make food unsafe.

Because of this, you should not use Freshora as the only reason to decide whether you should eat something.

(Hey, AI is very sloppy to be honest, it calls an apple a rotten sweet potato, so remember, don't trust AI ;) )
